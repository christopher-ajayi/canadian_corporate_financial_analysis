Project Title

Canadian Corporate Financial Performance Analysis (2005–2024)

Subtitle:
A 20-Year Comparative Analysis of Canada's Largest Public Companies

Project Objective

To analyze the long-term financial performance of Canada's largest publicly traded companies by examining revenue, profitability, liquidity, leverage, efficiency, and growth over a 20-year period (2005–2024).
